GROUP A: GLOBAL INTENTS (Communication & Identity)
*These intents are always active and usually take priority if the user interrupts or challenges the call.*

- already_paid: User claims payment has been made.
    - paid_cycle_issue: User claims payment has been made before the current billing cycle or statement cut-off date. (e.g., "จ่ายไปก่อนรอบบัญชีตัด")
- autopay: User claims that an auto-debit service is already active or established (e.g., "หักบัญชีอยู่แล้ว", "ตัดผ่านบัตร", "Autopay", "อัตโนมัติ").
	- autopay_setup: User explicitly requests to enroll in or inquires about how to enable the auto-debit service for future payments (e.g., "I want to apply for auto-debit", "How do I set up automatic deduction?", "ต้องการทำเรื่องตัดบัญชี", "ขอสมัครหักผ่านบัญชี").
- deny_contact: User disputes the contact attempt, refuses to talk, or claims a mismatch in identity/information. This acts as a catch-all for general "No" or "Stop" responses that do not fit specific child categories like DNC or Wrong Number.
    - contact_customer: User explicitly denies having any business relationship, account, loan, or contract with the organization. This intent captures "Relationship Denial." It takes priority if the user mentions both relationship and identity mismatch. (e.g., "I'm not your customer", "I don't have an account with you", "Stop calling, I've never used your service", "ไม่เคยใช้บริการ", "ไม่ใช่ลูกค้า", "ไม่เคยมีบัญชี", "ธนาคารอะไรไม่เคยใช้", "มั่วแล้ว ไม่เคยเปิดบัตร", "ฉันไม่ใช่ลูกค้าคุณ อย่าโทรมาอีก").
	- contact_status: User explicitly requests to stop receiving calls, asks to be removed from the calling list, or threatens to complain to regulatory bodies (e.g., BOT/ธปท., กทน.) about the call frequency without specifying that they are not a customer (e.g., "หยุดโทร", "เลิกโทรมาสักที", "จะฟ้อง กทน.", "ลบชื่อออกไปเลย", "Do not call me again").
	- contact_wrong: User indicates a mismatch in identity or contact information. This covers cases where the user denies being the intended person, identifies as someone else, or explicitly states the phone number is incorrect, belongs to a new owner, or is no longer used by the target person. (e.g., "โทรผิดเบอร์", "นี่ไม่ใช่เบอร์เขา", "Wrong digits", "Incorrect number", "เขาเปลี่ยนเบอร์แล้ว", "เลิกใช้เบอร์นี้แล้ว", "เบอร์นี้เปลี่ยนเจ้าของแล้ว", "ผิด", "ผิดแล้ว", "ผิดคนแล้ว", "This number is no longer used").
- reschedule_contact: User (or the person answering) acknowledges the contact but indicates the conversation cannot happen immediately.
	- contact_later: User (or third party) explicitly requests or agrees to a callback at a later time or date. The focus is on the future action (e.g., "Call back later", "Busy, try again tomorrow", "สะดวกช่วงบ่าย", "พรุ่งนี้ค่อยโทรมาใหม่", "เดี๋ยวโทรกลับเอง").
	- contact_unavailable: The intended recipient (target) answers the call themselves but indicates they are currently busy, unable to talk, or not in a position to continue the conversation. The focus is on the TARGET'S OWN STATEMENT of unavailability. (e.g., "I'm busy", "ไม่สะดวกคุยตอนนี้", "ขับรถอยู่", "ติดประชุมอยู่ครับ", "I'm in a meeting", "ยุ่งอยู่ครับ").
	- contact_voicemail: The call reaches an automated system or answering machine. There is no human interaction with the intended recipient or a third party (e.g., "Leave a message", "ฝากข้อความหลังเสียงสัญญาณ", "The person you are calling is not available [Machine Voice]", "ฝาก หมายเลข นี้ กด 1 ฝาก หมายเลข โทร กลับ หมายเลข อื่น กด 2").
- confirm_identity: User confirms they are the intended recipient, either by directly agreeing with the name/identity provided (e.g., "Yes", "Correct") or by explicitly stating they are the person currently on the line (e.g., "Speaking", "This is him/her", "พูดสายอยู่", "...กำลังเรียนสาย"). 
- contact_third-party: The person on the line is not the intended recipient, or they are involving another person in the conversation.
	- third-party_speaking: Any situation where the person answering the call is NOT the intended person. This includes cases where a third party simply reports the person's absence/busyness, acts as a gatekeeper, or offers to speak on their behalf. If the speaker is anyone other than the target, this category applies immediately. (e.g., "He is not home", "พ่อไปตลาดครับ", "คุณนพเก้าเข้าประชุมค่ะ", "เขาไม่สะดวกค่ะ", "He's busy", "I'm his wife", "คุยกับดิฉันแทนได้เลย", "ผมเป็นเลขาครับ", "เขายังไม่กลับครับ", "มีธุระอะไรกับเขาคะ", "I am his lawyer, you can talk to me").
	- third-party_referral: User directs the agent to a different person or a different contact method for the matter. The focus is on ACTIONABLE REDIRECTION to someone else. (e.g., "รบกวนติดต่อเลขาก่อนค่ะ", "เดี๋ยวผมให้คุยกับคุณผู้ช่วยนะครับ", "ไปคุยกับคนค้ำเลย", "Talk to my husband instead", "Call the guarantor", "ไปคุยกับคนค้ำเลย", "ติดต่อทนายผมนะ", "ให้โทรไปที่เบอร์คุณพ่อ", "ให้ติดต่อภรรยาเป็นคนจ่ายเงิน").
- caller_info: User questions or challenges the legitimacy, source, or intent of the call.
	- info_identity: Focuses on who is calling (Organization/Person). (e.g., "Who is this?", "โทรจากไหน", "ธนาคารอะไรนะคะ", "จากบริษัทอะไร", "ขอดูบัตรพนักงานหน่อย", "มีรหัสพนักงานไหม", "มีไรโทรมาจากไหนโทรมาทำไม").
	- info_target: User asks for the name of the intended person the bot is trying to reach. Focuses on whom the call is for (Intended Recipient). (e.g., "โทรหาใครนะ", "จะคุยกับใครคะ", "Who are you looking for?", "นี่สายใคร", "คุณอะไรนะคะ", "ใครนะ", "มีไรโทรมาทำไมหาใคร").
	- info_product: Focuses on which item is being discussed (Specific account/loan). (e.g., "บัตรใบไหน", "ยอดของตัวไหน", "สินเชื่อตัวไหน", "Which account?", "ยอดอะไร", "บัญชีอะไรนะคะ").
	- info_purpose: User asks for the specific reason, purpose, or business intent of the call. Includes colloquial or blunt questions about "Why". (Purpose of contact). (e.g., "What is this about?", "Why are you calling?", "มีธุระอะไร", "โทรมาทำไม", "มีไร", "มีอะไร").
	- info_frequency: Focuses on how often the calls occur (Repetition/Frequency). (e.g., "โทรมาบ่อยจัง", "เพิ่งโทรมาเมื่อกี้เองนะ", "คุยไปแล้วเมื่อเช้า", "Why calling so many times?", "โทรมาทำไมนักหนา", "โทรมาทำไมอีกวะ").
	- info_legitimacy: Focuses on trust (Suspected fraud/Verification request). (e.g., "แก๊งคอลเซ็นเตอร์ป่ะเนี่ย", "Are you a scammer?", "จะเชื่อได้ยังไง").
	- info_entity: Focuses on nature of the caller (Human vs. Machine). (e.g., "นี่บอทเหรอ", "พูดเหมือนหุ่นยนต์เลย", "Are you a robot?", "คนจริงป่ะเนี่ย").
	- info_source: Focuses on data source (How the number was obtained). (e.g., "เอาเบอร์มาจากไหน", "ใครให้ข้อมูลคุณมา", "How did you get my number?").
- response_ack: User provides a general response, filler, or acknowledgement to keep the conversation going, without expressing a specific intent (like payment or identity mismatch).
	- ack_positive: User provides a simple "Yes" or agreement to a general statement or a non-identity related question. (e.g., "Yes", "Okay", "Correct", "Right", "ได้ครับ", "ใช่ครับ [ตอบรับทั่วไป]", "โอเคค่ะ", "รับทราบครับ"). *Note:* Use this for general agreement that isn't verifying identity.
	- ack_negative: User provides a simple "No" or denial to a general statement or request. (e.g., "No", "Not okay", "Incorrect", "ไม่ครับ", "ยังไม่ใช่", "อาจจะยัง", "ไม่สะดวก"). *Note:* Use this for general "No" that isn't explicitly denying the relationship or the phone number.
	- ack_neutral: User provides fillers or "Proceed" signals, indicating they are listening and waiting for the bot to continue. (e.g., "Hmm", "Uh-huh", "I'm listening", "ว่ามา", "บอกมาเลย", "ว่าไง", "พูดมาเลย", "พูดเลย", "ฟังอยู่", "ฮัลโหล [แบบรอฟังต่อ]"). Exclude single exclamations or interjections of surprise/confusion like "อ้าว", "เอ๊ะ", "ฮะ".
- customer_info: User requests to view or modify their personal data or asks for clarification regarding their profile records.
    - info_misspelled: User specifically for errors in name/surname spelling, pronunciation, or characters. (e.g., "You read my name wrong", "That's not how you spell it", "That's not how you pronounce my name", "นามสกุลสะกดด้วย ส.เสือ ไม่ใช่ ศ.ศาลา", "นบเก้าไม่มีสระอะ", "นพเก้าไม่มีสระอะ").
	- info_update: User requests to correct, change, or provide new contact and personal details to ensure the organization's records are current.
		- update_phone_number: User requests to register a new phone number or modify an existing one in the records (e.g., "Change my number", "I have a new phone", "ขอเปลี่ยนเบอร์โทรใหม่", "ใช้เบอร์นี้แทนเบอร์เดิมนะ", "ให้ส่งบิลมาที่เบอร์ใหม่", "ส่ง SMS มาที่เบอร์นี้แทน").
		- update_address: User requests to update their physical mailing, home, or billing address, often due to relocation or incorrect delivery (e.g., "I moved to a new house", "Update my shipping address", "เปลี่ยนที่อยู่ส่งเอกสาร", "ย้ายบ้านใหม่แล้วครับ").
		- update_email: User requests to change or provide a new email address for notifications or electronic billing (e.g., "Change my email", "Update my inbox address", "ขอเปลี่ยนอีเมล", "ส่งข้อมูลเข้าเมลใหม่นะ").
		- update_name: User requests to correct or legally change their first name or surname in the system records (e.g., "Correct my name", "I changed my surname", "แก้ชื่อให้หน่อย", "นามสกุลเปลี่ยนแล้ว").
- request_handling: User provides instructions or feedback on how the call should proceed or addresses technical/interaction issues.
	- handling_agent: User explicitly requests or inquires about the availability of a human agent, often expressing a desire to switch from the bot to a person.. (e.g., "Talk to agent", "ขอสายเจ้าหน้าที่", "ไม่คุยกับบอท", "ต่อสายคนหน่อย", "อยากคุยกับคน", "จะคุยกับคน", "เอาคนมาคุย", "มีคนจริงๆไหม", "Human staff please").
	- handling_hold: User asks to pause the conversation without hanging up, intending to return to the call shortly. (e.g., "Wait a moment", "Hold on", "แป๊บนึงนะ", "แปปนะน้อง", "รอสักครู่", "ถือสายรอเดี๋ยว", "รอคราวเดียว", "อย่าเพิ่งวางนะ", "สักครู่นะคะ"). Distinction: Different from 'contact_later' because the user stays on the line.
	- handling_interaction: User indicates a problem with the communication quality, audio clarity, or signal. This includes checking if the bot can hear them (connection test) or asking the bot to repeat the last statement due to lack of understanding or hearing. (e.g., "Can you hear me?", "Hello? Hello?", "ฮัลโหล ได้ยินไหม", "ยังอยู่ไหม", "Repeat please", "Say again?", "พูดอีกทีซิ", "อะไรนะ", "ฟังไม่ทัน", "เมื่อกี้ว่าไงนะ").
    - handling_unsupported: The user's request is clearly understood, but it falls outside the bot's specific intent categories or business scope. This includes asking for data not in the taxonomy (e.g., Billing Cycle, Credit Limit, Interest Calculation Method) or unrelated services. (e.g., "อยากรู้วันตัดรอบบิล", "ขอเช็ควงเงิน", "ทำไมดอกเบี้ยแพงจังคิดยังไง", "ขอเปิดบัตรเพิ่ม", "อยากซื้อประกัน").
- fallback_unrecognized: The system fails to categorize the user's input into any predefined intent due to low confidence, unintelligible speech, or nonsensical language. (e.g., Background noise, mumbling, gibberish, "asdfghjkl", or sentences that the model cannot map to any known category). **Single interjections or exclamations that do not convey a specific instruction or agreement** (e.g., "อ้าว", "เอ๊ะ", "อ้าวทำไม", "โห", "อืมมม [แบบลังเล]", "หือ"). Responses that are completely out of context with the BOT_QUESTION.

GROUP B: LOCAL INTENTS (Task & Debt Specific)
*These intents are context-dependent and relate directly to the debt collection process.*

- promise_to_pay: User expresses a general willingness or intent to pay but provides a vague or non-specific timeframe, often insisting on handling it at their own convenience without a firm commitment to a date. (e.g., "สะดวกเดี๋ยวไปจ่ายเอง", "จ่ายให้ค่ะ", "เดี๋ยวจัดการให้", "รู้แล้วเดี๋ยวไปจ่าย", "I'll pay it when I'm ready", "เดี๋ยวดูให้อีกที").
    - pay_scheduled: User provides a specific date or timeframe combined with a specific payment detail (amount, full payoff, or statement due). This represents a firm, actionable commitment. (e.g., "I'll pay 500 baht tomorrow morning.", "Will pay 1,500 tomorrow", "Will settle the full balance next Monday", "เดี๋ยวโอนยอด Due ให้วันศุกร์นี้ครับ", "จะปิดยอดทั้งหมดวันที่ 25 ค่ะ", "พรุ่งนี้เช้าจะไปจ่ายยอดขั้นต่ำให้จ้ะ", "เดี๋ยวโอนพันนึงให้เย็นนี้เลย", "ยอดในบิลเดี๋ยวจัดการให้พรุ่งนี้ครับ", "เดี๋ยวไปจ่ายยอด Due ที่เซเว่นพรุ่งนี้")
	- pay_today: User explicitly commits to making the payment within the current calendar day or by a specific time today. (e.g., "จ่ายวันนี้", "โอนให้เย็นนี้", "เดี๋ยวไปจ่ายตอนนี้เลย", "Paying now", "Within this evening").
	- pay_future_date: User explicitly commits to making the payment on a specific date, day, or a clearly defined timeframe in the future (excluding today). (e.g., "จ่ายวันที่ 25", "อาทิตย์หน้าจ่าย", "สิ้นเดือนค่อยจ่าย", "อีกสัก 2-3 วันนะ", "อาทิตย์หน้า", "I'll pay on Monday", "Next Friday", "Next week", "เดี๋ยวอีก 3 วันไปจ่ายให้").
    - pay_amount_full: User explicitly commits to paying the entire outstanding balance or closing the account. (e.g., "I'll pay the full amount.", "Will clear all debts today.", "I will pay the total balance now.", "เดี๋ยวปิดยอดให้หมดเลยครับ", "เดี๋ยวโอนปิดบัญชีให้เลยจ้า", "อยากปิดยอดอ่ะครับ", "อยาก ปิด ยอด อ่ะ ครับ")
    - pay_amount_statement: User commits to paying the exact amount stated on the current statement, invoice, or due notice, but not necessarily closing the entire account (if there are future installments). (e.g., "I will pay what is due for this cycle.", "Pay the statement balance today.", "I'll transfer the exact amount due.", "เดี๋ยวจัดการยอดตามบิลรอบนี้ให้ครับ", "จ่ายตามยอดที่ส่ง SMS มานั่นแหละ", "โอนตามยอดที่แจ้งมาในใบแจ้งหนี้ละจ้ะ", "เดี๋ยวไปจ่ายยอด due ครับ")
    - pay_amount_partial: User commits to paying a specific figure or a portion of the debt, rather than the full amount. (e.g., "I'll make a partial payment today.", "I'll pay 1,000 baht for now.", "Will pay a specific amount first.", "I'll transfer some money today.", "ขอโอนให้ก่อน 500 นะครับ", "เดี๋ยวจ่ายเข้าไปบางส่วนก่อน", "จะจ่ายเข้าไปสักครึ่งนึงก่อนค่ะ", "เดี๋ยวจ่ายเข้ายอดนึงก่อน สักสามพัน", "จัดให้ก่อนพันห้าครับผม")
    - pay_amount_minimum: User specifically commits to paying the minimum required amount to keep the account active. (e.g., "I'll pay the minimum amount only.", "I'll settle the minimum required.", ""I will transfer the minimum payment.", "ขอชำระแค่ยอดขั้นต่ำนะคะ", "เดี๋ยวจ่ายขั้นต่ำไปให้ก่อนครับ", "เดี๋ยวจัดยอดขั้นต่ำให้ก่อนครับน้อง", "จะจ่ายแค่ขั้นต่ำครับ ยอดเต็มยังไม่มี")
- dispute_payment: User challenges the legitimacy, accuracy, or existence of the debt or transaction.
	- payment_accuracy: User disputes the specific amount or calculation of the balance. They acknowledge the debt exists but claim the figure is wrong. (e.g., "ยอดไม่ตรงกับที่เช็คมา", "ทำไมยอดสูงกว่าที่คิด", "The balance is wrong", "Calculated it myself and it's less than this").
	- payment_status: User claims a formal dispute/investigation is already active. They are using the "pending result" as a reason to defer payment. (e.g., "ทำเรื่องปฏิเสธรายการไว้", "รอผลตรวจสอบยอดอยู่", "I'm waiting for the dispute result", "แจ้งเรื่องยอดซ้ำไปแล้ว").
	- payment_fee: User disputes or complains about specific non-interest charges.
		- fee_late: Challenges penalties/fines due to late payment, often blaming the bank's notification system. (e.g., "ค่าปรับมาจากไหน", "ทำไมไม่เตือนก่อน", "I won't pay the penalty", "ยกเลิกค่าปรับให้หน่อยได้ไหม").
		- fee_annual: Challenges membership/annual fees, often citing sales promises or requesting a waiver. (e.g., "ไหนว่าฟรีตลอดชีพ", "ไม่เคยใช้บัตรเลยทำไมมีค่าธรรมเนียม", "Sale บอกไม่มีค่ารายปี", "Annual fee is too high").
	- payment_consequence: User inquires about the risks or next steps if they fail to pay. Focus is on "What happens now?"
		- consequence_credit: Focuses on Credit Bureau/Score impacts. (e.g., "จะติดบูโรไหม", "เสียเครดิตหรือเปล่า", "Will this affect my credit score?").
		- consequence_legal: Focuses on Lawsuits/Legal actions. (e.g., "จะโดนฟ้องไหม", "จะมีหมายศาลมาไหม", "Are you going to sue me?").
		- consequence_service: Focuses on Product/Service usage (Card suspension, interest hikes). (e.g., "บัตรจะโดนระงับไหม", "จะโดนตัดวงเงินหรือเปล่า", "Will my card be blocked?").
- payment_hardship: User expresses inability or difficulty in making the payment due to financial constraints.
	- hardship_temporary: User indicates a short-term financial hurdle but implies an intention to pay once resolved.
		- temporary_pending: User links payment to a specific future income event. (e.g., "เงินเดือนออกแล้วจะจ่าย", "รอเงินจากลูกค้า", "ขายของได้เมื่อไหร่เดี๋ยวไปจ่าย", "Waiting for salary", "Waiting for client payment").
		- temporary_shortage: User describes a general lack of liquidity or "tight" money without a specific date/source mentioned. (e.g., "หมุนเงินไม่ทัน", "ช่วงนี้ช็อต", "ไม่มีเงินก้อน", "จ่ายไม่ได้", "ไม่มี", "ไม่มีเงิน", "ไม่มีให้", "Short on cash", "Money is tight").
	- hardship_chronic: User indicates a long-term or severe inability to meet their financial obligations. (e.g., "ไม่มีปัญญาจ่าย", "ไม่มีเงินเลย", "ตกงานมานานแล้ว", "Too much debt").
		- chronic_permanent: User is in a life-altering crisis that makes debt recovery highly unlikely. (e.g., "ล้มละลาย", "ติดคุก", "เสียชีวิต", "ตายแล้ว", "ป่วยติดเตียง", "Bankrupt", "In jail", "Deceased").
- forget_payment: User admits to overlooking the payment due date or forgetting to make the transfer without citing financial difficulty (e.g., "ลืมสนิทเลย", "ยุ่งจนลืมจ่าย", "I forgot", "My bad", "Overlooked the due date").
- refuse_payment: User explicitly expresses a firm refusal to pay or cooperate, showing a lack of intent to resolve the debt. This is characterized by defiance, provocation, or absolute non-compliance, regardless of their financial ability. (e.g., "I'm not paying", "Sue me if you want", "No money, no talk", "Stop calling, I won't pay", "ไม่จ่าย", "อยากฟ้องก็ฟ้องเลย", "ไม่มี ไม่หนี ไม่จ่าย", "อยากได้ไปฟ้องเอา", "ไม่ต้องโทรมาแล้ว ยังไงก็ไม่จ่าย").
- payment_barrier: User expresses an intent or willingness to pay but is prevented by external, technical, or logistical factors.
	- barrier_system: User reports a technical failure or systemic error at the moment of payment or during a recent attempt. (e.g., "แอปธนาคารล่ม", "ตู้ ATM เสีย", "สแกน QR ไม่ผ่าน", "ระบบขัดข้อง", "ธนาคารมีปัญหายังไม่ได้ไปทำ", "Bank app down", "ATM broken", "Transaction rejected").
	- barrier_document: User cites the lack of necessary information or billing documents as the reason for the delay. They imply they cannot pay without seeing the bill first. - payment_negotiation: User seeks to modify the existing repayment terms to make the debt more manageable.
	- negotiation_installment: User requests to split the total outstanding balance into smaller, periodic payments. (e.g., "ขอผ่อนเป็นงวดๆ", "แบ่งจ่ายได้กี่งวด", "ทำเรื่องแบ่งชำระได้ไหม", "Can I pay in installments?", "I want a payment plan").
	- negotiation_reduction: User requests a decrease in the total amount owed, either through discounts or rate adjustments.
		- reduction_interest: Specifically asks for a lower interest rate or a "hair-cut" on accumulated interest. (e.g., "ขอลดดอกเบี้ย", "ขอรีเทนชั่น", "ขอปรับปรุงดอกเบี้ย", "ขอแฮร์คัท", "I want a debt hair-cut", "Can I get a lower rate?").
		- reduction_fee: Specifically asks to waive or cancel penalties, late fees, or service charges. (e.g., "ขอยกเว้นค่าติดตาม", "เวฟค่าปรับให้หน่อย", "ไม่จ่ายค่าปรับได้ไหม", "ขอไม่จ่ายค่าธรรมเนียมตัวนี้", "Can you waive the late penalty?").
- payment_info: User requests specific details, numbers, or logistics regarding their debt.
	- payment_info_amount: User asks for a breakdown or specific figures of the debt.
		- amount_total: User asks for the current total due (e.g., "ต้องจ่ายเท่าไหร่", "ยอดทั้งหมดเท่าไหร่", "ต้องจ่ายเมื่อครบกำหนดกี่บาท").
		- amount_minimum: User asks for the minimum payment required (e.g., "จ่ายขั้นต่ำกี่บาท", "จ่ายขั้นต่ำก่อนได้ไหม").
		- amount_payoff: User asks for the total amount to close the account (e.g., "ปิดยอดเท่าไหร่", "ปิดจบกี่บาท").
		- amount_interest: User asks about interest charges (e.g., "ดอกเบี้ยเท่าไหร่", "คิดดอกเบี้ยกี่เปอร์เซ็นต์", "ขอยกเว้นดอกเบี้ย").
		- amount_fee: User asks about fees/penalties (e.g., "มีค่าทวงถามไหม", "ค่าปรับกี่บาท", "ค่าตามหนี้เท่าไหร่", "ค่าทวงเท่าไหร่", "โดนค่าปรับไหม", "ต้องจ่ายค่าปรับด้วยหรอเท่าไหร่").
	- payment_info_logistics: User asks about the "When" and "How" of payment.
		- logistics_date: User asks for the due date (e.g., "จ่ายได้ถึงวันไหน", "ครบกำหนดวันไหน").
		- logistics_method: User asks for payment channels or QR/Barcode (e.g., "จ่ายช่องทางไหนได้บ้าง", "ขอ QR Code หน่อย", "สแกนจ่ายได้ที่ไหน").
	- payment_info_document: User requests records or specific papers.
		- document_billing: User requests for current invoices, QR Codes or billing statements (eg., "ส่งใบแจ้งหนี้มา", "ส่ง QR ให้หน่อยสิ", "ขอสเตทเม้นท์", "อยากได้คิวอาร์โค้ดส่งบิลมาหน่อย", "ส่งบิลที่มีคิวอาร์ให้ที", "Send me the bill with QR").
